How to write this honestly

Seven weeks of individual builds are about to become organizational decisions — what AI infrastructure we invest in, what enablement we run again, what we ignore. The success metric flips this week. We're not scoring application volume or build polish. We're scoring how clearly you see where AI fits in your work and where it doesn't. The strongest brief in this cohort will probably come from someone who shipped fewer tools but understood their failure modes more clearly than anyone else.

Be honest. A lot of what got built across these weeks was for the program — for the share-out, for the leaderboard, for the momentum of the cohort. Some of those tools will survive into your real work. Plenty won't. Calling that out cleanly is the assignment. Red is a strong tag. The judgment to mark your own demo performative is something we can't get from anywhere else.

The voice we want: skeptical, optimistic, and explorative — all three at once. Skeptical about your own builds. Optimistic about where AI is actually creating leverage. Explorative about what's worth trying next.

30–40 minutes. Tight bullets, not essays.

Why this matters

We ran this program because we think AI is one of the most important shifts hitting how we work, and we wanted everyone here to face it firsthand. Some of what you built across these eight weeks is going to outlast the program. Most of it isn't — and we knew that going in. What we want from the final week is your honest read on which is which, what we should do about it, and where the org should go from here.

Our best people get this kind of question right not by being the most enthusiastic AI adopters in the room, but by being the ones who can tell a real use case from a performative one, an optimistic bet from a hype cycle, a leverage point from a distraction. This brief is the one place you get to show that judgment cleanly. Use it.
